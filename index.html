Wedr – Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026

Wedr is a weather app and home-screen widget for Android. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short, it uses your location only to show the weather for where you are, and it has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking.
  • Your location is used only to get the weather, and only a rounded position (about 5 km) reaches the weather provider.
  • The microphone, if you allow it, is listened to on your phone only and is never recorded or sent anywhere.
  • Nothing about you is stored on any server, sold or shared for advertising.

Location

With your permission, Wedr reads your device's approximate location (it never asks for precise location) to show the weather where you are. The location is used as follows:

  • Weather. The app sends the coordinates to Wedr's own weather server (a small service run on Cloudflare). That server rounds them to a grid of about 5 km and asks OpenWeather for the weather at that rounded point. OpenWeather never receives your exact position or your IP address. The server does not store or log your location.
  • City name. To show the name of your town, the app uses Android's built-in geocoder, which may send the coordinates to Google to look up the place name. This is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • On your phone. The last weather, together with the location it was fetched for, is saved on your device so the app and the widget can show it straight away and without a connection. It is never included in backups.
  • Widget. The widget refreshes the weather from time to time using the last location your phone already knows. Wedr does not ask for background location access.

If you don't allow location, Wedr shows no weather and asks for it instead; it never guesses where you are. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your phone's settings.

Microphone

On snowy days you can blow at the screen to blow the snow away. For this, and only if you allow it, Wedr listens to the microphone while that screen is open. The sound is analysed on your phone, a few milliseconds at a time, only to tell whether you are blowing. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your phone's settings; the rest of the app works without it.

Data stored on your device

Wedr saves the last weather (with its location), whether it has already asked for location, and one setting: whether sound is on. The weather and its location stay on your device. If you use Android's backup, only the sound setting is included in your device backup to your Google account, or copied when you move to a new phone, so it carries over. Uninstalling Wedr, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, deletes all of it.

Services Wedr relies on

  • Cloudflare runs Wedr's weather server. Like any internet service, it handles your IP address to deliver the response. Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • OpenWeather provides the weather data and receives only the rounded location. OpenWeather Privacy Policy.
  • Google provides location services and the geocoder on Android, and distributes the app through Google Play. Google Privacy Policy.

Wedr does not sell your information and does not share it with anyone for advertising or any other purpose beyond what is described above.

Children

Wedr is a general weather app. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices and rights

Because Wedr keeps no information about you on any server, there is nothing held about you that could be accessed, corrected or deleted on request. You stay in control on your phone: you can deny or withdraw the location and microphone permissions, clear the app's data, or uninstall it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
